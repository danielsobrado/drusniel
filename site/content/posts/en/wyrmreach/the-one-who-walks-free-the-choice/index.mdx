## Chapter 13 | Part 3

---

![The choice to not run alone](./the-choice-to-not-run-alone_sora.jpg)

His magic returned on the fourth day.

Barely.

Drusniel waited until the guards were looking away, then reached for the faint air already moving through the cage.

He caught the current.

Narrowed it.

Fed a thread of pressure into the lock's keyway.

![A thread of air magic](./a-thread-of-air-magic_sora.jpg)

The pins answered through resistance.

Old lock.

Corroded.

Simple enough.

He could open it.

The cost would be most of what he had recovered.

Elion's lock would cost the rest.

Drusniel looked toward the third cage.

Nine prisoners.

One additional lock.

Seven guards.

Two locks were possible.

Three were not.

He hated the arithmetic.

It remained arithmetic.

He spent the day watching.

The evening guard favored his left knee.

Not hidden. Elion had noticed it too.

The driver of the third wagon checked beneath his seat every time another guard approached.

Keys, probably.

The fourth wagon smelled of lamp oil.

No borrowed memories required.

Just attention.

![Elion knows things](./elion-knows-things_sora.jpg)

Elion did have one memory he could not place.

Stone formations east of the road.

A narrow route between them.

He remembered running it.

He did not remember whose legs had carried him.

"Can you find it again?" Drusniel asked.

"Maybe."

"Useful."

"I told you. That's not the same as reliable."

Better answer.

When the deepest watch came, two guards remained awake.

Drusniel put his hand against his lock.

Caught the small current sliding between the bars.

Compressed it.

Pressed it into the keyway.

One pin.

Second.

Third.

Click.

The door opened.

![He opens his lock and runs](./he-opens-his-lock-and-runs_sora.jpg)

Freedom lay away from the wagons.

Elion's cage lay toward them.

The numbers said leave him.

Drusniel looked across the yard.

For once, he disliked the answer.

He ran toward Elion's cage.
