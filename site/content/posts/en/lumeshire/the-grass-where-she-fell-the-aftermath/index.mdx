## Chapter 12 | Part 1

---

Earlier that day, outside Riverhold, the first thing Maris felt was cold grass against her cheek.

Then mud between her fingers.

Then the ache in her jaw.

Maris Hale opened her eyes.

The sun had moved.

It had been morning when she was walking toward Riverhold. Now the light lay low across the field.

Hours gone.

Again.

She rolled onto one elbow.

Her skull objected.

Her tongue hurt too. She touched it carefully and tasted blood.

Bitten during the vision.

Not useful.

![Blood on fingers](./blood-on-fingers_sora.jpg)

Boat.

Black water.

A hand losing its grip.

She closed her eyes until the field stopped tilting.

Ground beneath her.

Wind on her face.

A lark calling somewhere to the left.

Ordinary things.

She got one knee under herself.

Then the other.

"Right," she said.

Her voice sounded terrible.

"That was fun."

![Riverhold in the distance](./riverhold-in-distance_sora.jpg)

Riverhold sat on the horizon, close enough to see roofs and smoke.

She did not remember leaving the road.

She did not remember entering the field.

She did not remember falling.

That was normal enough to be frightening.

The vision pressed at the edge of her thoughts.

Boat.

Black water.

The hand.

Usually the details broke apart after she woke. Color first. Then faces. Then sequence.

This one stayed.

Maris stood.

Her legs shook hard enough that she had to wait before moving.

She pressed her thumbnail into the old scar at the base of her palm.

Specific pain.

Specific place.

Here.

Now.

The field stayed a field.

Good.

She started toward Riverhold.

![Maris walks shakily](./maris-walks-shakily_sora.jpg)

The hand returned once while she walked.

Exactly the same.

That was new.
