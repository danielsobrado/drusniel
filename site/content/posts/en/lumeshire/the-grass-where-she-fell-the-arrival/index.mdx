## Chapter 12 | Part 6

---

The second door was at the end of the upstairs corridor.

Maris knew before she reached it.

The pressure behind her eyes sharpened into something almost audible.

Not words.

Not exactly sound.

A sustained demand.

She stopped six paces away.

Pressed her thumbnail into the scar on her palm.

Corridor.

Wooden floor.

Lamp smoke.

Voices behind the door.

Real.

Probably.

She took two more steps.

The pressure increased.

Four paces.

Then three.

Something beyond the door pulsed.

Maris flinched.

There.

Source.

Not explanation.

Source.

She heard several people speaking inside.

A man's voice, clipped.

An older voice answering.

Someone younger at the window, perhaps.

None of them sounded as though they expected her.

Good.

The stable image touched her mind.

Not the whole thing.

Only the hand.

Then it was gone.

Maris put her hand on the latch.

*At least drowning in someone else's black water was new.*

A laugh almost escaped.

She opened the door.
