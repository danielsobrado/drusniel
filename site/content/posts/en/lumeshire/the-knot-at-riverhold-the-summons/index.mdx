## Chapter 10 | Part 2

--- 

The room had three exits if Eldric counted the window.

He counted it.

Two dwarves sat beside a table.

The older one had a merchant's hands. The younger one's heel kept tapping against the floor.

Between them rested a metal cube.

![Cube resting on silk with an unsettling presence](./cube-on-silk_sora.jpg)

Eldric noticed it before Xandor said anything.

Not because it glowed.

Because his attention kept returning to it.

"You feel something," Xandor said.

"Insistence."

Xandor nodded once. "Good word. I don't know what causes it."

Better again.

"Dulint," Xandor said, "this is Eldric."

The older dwarf looked him over.

"You're the man who saw the Grukmar pattern."

"I'm the man nobody believed."

"Same man."

Eldric almost liked him.

Balin made an impatient sound.

"Can we get to the cube?"

Dulint looked at him.

"You've waited this long."

"Which is why I'm finished waiting."

Eldric stepped closer.

One face of the cube was faintly brighter than the others.

"Does it always orient like that?"

"Not always the same direction," Dulint said.

Xandor moved beside the table.

"Observed behavior first. It points. It changes temperature. Its intensity changes. Dulint and Balin were followed after it activated."

"Those are facts."

"Yes."

"And the rest?"

Xandor picked up a sheet covered in copied fragments.

"Textual evidence: one surviving source describes a Nexus function translated as the **Sense phase**. The translation may be approximate."

Balin folded his arms.

"And what does Sense do?"

"That is where fact ends."

![Xandor explains the Nexus and barrier mechanics](./xandor-explains_sora.jpg)

Xandor tapped the page.

"The text associates Sense with locating what it calls resonance. It does not define resonance."

"Artifacts?" Eldric asked.

"Possibly."

"People?"

"Possibly."

"Places?"

"Possibly. But if I say yes to everything, the word becomes useless."

Balin pointed at the cube.

"So what do we actually know?"

Dulint answered.

"It points. People found us."

"Can you hide it?"

"We tried wrapping it and burying it," Dulint said. "The followers still went straight toward it."

That interested Eldric more than any ancient terminology.

"Can you destroy it?"

Dulint's expression tightened. "Haven't found a method I'd trust to fail safely."

"Turn it off?"

Xandor shook his head.

"No demonstrated control."

Eldric looked at the cube again.

"So your working hypothesis is that Sense looks for this resonance and leaks enough of its own activity for other interested parties to follow."

Xandor smiled faintly.

"Working hypothesis. Yes."

"Local range?"

"Unknown."

"Who can detect it?"

"Unknown."

"Why does it point?"

"Unknown."

Balin looked at his uncle.

"I like him."

Dulint grunted. "He says 'unknown' faster."

Eldric counted the window again.

"Tell me about the pursuit."

That was a problem he understood.
