## Chapter 12 | Part 5

---

The Scholar's Rest looked disappointingly ordinary.

Blue shutters.

Stone walls.

Warm windows.

A painted sign with a book on it.

Maris stood across the street.

The pressure behind her eyes had become a steady throb.

She could leave.

That remained true.

She checked it carefully.

Turn south.

Walk to the gate.

Find another road.

The visions would probably follow.

They usually did.

The headaches certainly would.

Maris crossed the street.

Inside, the common room smelled of stew and wet wool.

No old scholar surrounded by ritual plants.

No waiting prophet.

Just travelers eating dinner.

Better.

"Can I help you?" a serving woman asked.

"I'm looking for Xandor."

The woman glanced toward the stairs.

"Upstairs. Second door. He's with visitors."

Visitors.

Maris almost asked who.

Then didn't.

The pressure in her skull strengthened when she looked toward the stairs.

So did the certainty that something above was very loud in a way sound was not supposed to be.

She hated that sentence even inside her own head.

"Wonderful," she muttered.

The serving woman raised an eyebrow.

"Long day."

"Looks like it."

Maris put one foot on the stairs.

Then stopped.

![Maris makes her choice](./maris-choice-made_sora.jpg)

She did not want a calling.

She did not want a craft.

She did not want someone else explaining what category of strange woman she belonged to.

She wanted the pressure to stop.

She wanted to know why the same hand kept slipping beneath black water.

That was enough.

She climbed.
