## Chapter 12 | Part 4

---

An old man stepped into Maris's path.

"Coin for the unfortunate?"

His beard had collected half of Riverhold.

His hand trembled.

Maris gave him a copper.

"For food."

"Of course."

His fingers closed around the coin.

The trembling stopped.

![The coin triggers the transformation](./coin-transformation_sora.jpg)

Maris stopped breathing.

The man's eyes focused on her.

Not warmly.

Not coldly.

Simply all at once.

"The Scholar's Rest," he said.

His voice no longer sounded like his.

"Ask for Xandor."

That was all.

![The beggar delivers the message](./beggar-prophecy_sora.jpg)

Maris waited.

The old man blinked.

His hand started trembling again.

He looked at the copper.

"Bless you, ma'am."

Then he shuffled away.

![The beggar wanders off](./beggar-wanders-off_sora.jpg)

Maris watched until the crowd swallowed him.

Not the first stranger whose mouth had spoken to her in someone else's voice.

Still unpleasant.

She did not know who had spoken.

She did not know why.

She did not know whether Xandor was a person, title, trap or joke.

Better.

Questions she could recognize as questions.

The pressure behind her eyes pulled toward the next street.

Maris followed it.

At a baker's stall she asked for the Scholar's Rest.

"Two streets north. Blue shutters."

No prophecy required.

Maris continued.

The stable image did not return.

That worried her more than if it had.
