## Chapter 10 | Part 3

--- 

They gave Eldric the pursuit from Stonehold onward.

He rebuilt it as a campaign map.

First contact after activation.

Four pursuers.

No interest in tracks when the cube had been hidden beneath the culvert.

Direct movement toward the buried object.

Then adaptation after Dulint recovered it.

"That part matters," Eldric said.

"Why?" Balin asked.

"They weren't following you. They were following the cube."

Dulint nodded.

"That's what we concluded."

"Good. Conclusions are cheaper when someone else pays for the experiment."

Balin gave him a look.

Eldric ignored it.

Xandor had arranged his notes in three columns.

**Observed. Text. Hypothesis.**

Eldric approved.

![Sense mode beacon pulse](./sense-mode_sora.jpg)

Under *Observed*:

The cube oriented.

It heated.

Pursuers could relocate it after concealment.

Under *Text*:

**Sense phase. Resonance.**

Under *Hypothesis*:

The cube searched for resonance and exposed enough of its own activity to be tracked.

Nothing more.

"Since Riverhold?" Eldric asked.

Dulint pointed north.

"Mostly that direction. Frostgard, perhaps. But it started shifting this afternoon."

"How?"

"Small corrections. Then larger ones."

"Moving target."

"Maybe," Xandor said.

Eldric looked at him.

"Good."

"What?"

"You didn't say yes."

Xandor tapped the hypothesis column.

"Evidence first."

The cube shifted.

All four men noticed.

Its brighter face moved several degrees toward Eldric.

Then stopped.

Balin looked between them.

"Well."

Eldric moved left.

The cube did not follow.

He moved right.

Nothing.

"Not tracking me."

Xandor frowned.

"It shifted when you entered yesterday."

"Or while I entered."

Eldric looked behind himself.

Door.

Stairs beyond it.

"If something was approaching from that direction, I would have stood between the cube and it."

Xandor stared at the orientation.

"Possible."

"Better than deciding I'm magical because I count exits."

Balin snorted.

Dulint hid a smile.

The cube shifted again.

Toward the door.

Eldric's hand settled near his knife.

"How long until whatever that is reaches us?"

"We don't know," Xandor said.

"Then we decide whether to move."

"We tried," Dulint said. "Main northern routes are watched."

"Minor roads?"

"With the cube exposing us?"

Eldric considered the map.

"Trouble already owns the roads."

Balin leaned against the window.

"So we wait?"

"We prepare," Eldric said. "Waiting is what people call preparation when they haven't done any."

He put Balin at the window.

Dulint beside the cube.

Xandor with his notes.

Eldric took the door.

The cube moved another fraction toward him.

No.

Toward whatever was behind him.

"Now," he said, "we find out whether that distinction matters."
