## Chapter 5 | Part 2
--- 

The presence found him that night.

He shifted the pillow beneath his aching head and reached toward the familiar pressure.

*Drus. Are you alright? I sensed something... wrong.*

*Your house,* Drusniel sent. *Vrinn. What is your father doing?*

*You know what isolation means. No visits from home.*

*Then how can you help me?*

*There are requisitions in the lower council annex. Two mercenary companies. Both marked urgent.*

![Shyntara watching from the shadows](./shyntara-watching_sora.jpg)


Drusniel sat up in bed. His heart thudded against his ribs. *How do you know?*

![Distorted voice warning in the night](./voice-distortion_sora.jpg)


*Logistics copies reach the annex before the houses receive their summaries.*


*Which file?*


*Recent dispatches. Start with the receipt ledger.*

*Which companies?*

*The copies list one as surface-born, one mixed. No company names.*

*Dates?*

*Not exact. But the requisitions were marked urgent.*

*They're spying on us.*

*The phrase to look for is "Thel'varin solution."*

*What comes before it?*


*That's all I have for you.*


The family knew about scouts and patrol mapping. They had not mentioned mercenaries or requisitions.

*How recent?*

*Days. Not weeks.*

*I'll verify the requisitions.*

*Good. Stay close to your family. Keep training if you can.*

*With Zaelar.*

*If he has something useful to teach you.*

*I decide what's useful.*

*Of course. I want you to survive this, Drus.*

*Then bring me something I can check.*

*Look in the annex.* The contact weakened.

The presence withdrew.

Drusniel rubbed his forehead until he could bear to light a lamp.

He wrote down the file and the phrase. If he took only a warning to his father, he would be asked where he'd heard it.


He would check the receipt ledger in the morning.
