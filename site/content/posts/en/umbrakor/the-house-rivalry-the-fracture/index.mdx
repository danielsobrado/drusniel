## Chapter 5 | Part 2
--- 

The presence found him that night.

Drusniel lay in darkness, unable to sleep, when the familiar warmth brushed against his awareness. The texture imitated Annariel well enough to hurt.

*Drus. Are you alright? I sensed something... wrong.*

He reached toward the connection. *House Vrinn. My father says they're making moves against us.*

A pause. Then, carefully: *I've heard things.*

*What things?*

*Names. Fragments of dates, I think—the reports were coded.* The presence pulsed with urgency. *I can't say more—the training halls have ears, and they might be monitoring mental connections. But something about your family has been circulating. I've seen indirect references. Vrinn-aligned mages discussing Thel'varin territory.*

![Shyntara watching from the shadows](./shyntara-watching_sora.jpg)


Drusniel sat up in bed. His heart thudded against his ribs. *How do you know?*

![Distorted voice warning in the night](./voice-distortion_sora.jpg)


*I passed the trials. Some reports reach the training halls before they are summarized for the houses.*

Drusniel's thumb stopped against his fingertips.

*Where did you see the requisitions? Which archive?*

A pause.

*Lower council annex. Logistics copies, not originals. I only saw fragments.*

*Names?*

*Two mercenary companies. One surface-born. One mixed. I didn't recognize either.*

*Dates?*

*Not exact. But the requisitions were marked urgent.*

*They're spying on us.*

*There was also a reference to a "Thel'varin solution." I don't know what it means.*

*Quote it.*

Another pause.

*That was the phrase. Nothing more.*

Drusniel counted four breaths.

The family knew about scouts and patrol mapping. They had not mentioned mercenaries or requisitions.

*How recent?*

*Days. Not weeks.*

*I'll verify the requisitions.*

*Good. Stay close to your family. Keep training if you can.*

*With Zaelar.*

*Yes. But don't mistake usefulness for trust—not with Zaelar, not with me, not with anyone.*

*I will.*

*I couldn't bear to lose you too.* The connection tightened around the words. *You're the only person who knew me before all of this.*

*I'll be careful,* Drusniel sent. *I promise.*

*Good.* The warmth began to fade. *I'll keep listening.*

The presence withdrew.

Drusniel lay in darkness.

Mercenaries. Urgent requisitions. A phrase he could not verify.

Three claims.

By morning, he intended to test at least one.
