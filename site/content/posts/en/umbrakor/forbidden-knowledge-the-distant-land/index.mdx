## Chapter 4 | Part 4
--- 

The map covered Zaelar's entire desk.

Drusniel had never seen anything like it—not a map of tunnels or caverns, but of the surface world, stretching across distances he couldn't comprehend. Coastlines. Mountains. Forests. And at the eastern edge, beyond a stretch of water marked with warning symbols, a landmass labeled in faded ink.

"Wyrmreach," Zaelar said, tracing the coastline with one long finger. "The land beyond the barrier."

![Zaelar pointing at Wyrmreach on the map](./pointing-finger_sora.jpg)

"I know what Wyrmreach is." Drusniel kept his distance from the desk. "Every drow child learns the stories. The nightmare sea. The barrier. The realm of dragons. No one who crosses returns."

"No *ordinary* drow returns." Zaelar looked up. "But you're not ordinary, are you?"

"Why are you showing me this?"

"Because I need a courier." Zaelar straightened, hands folded behind his back. "Someone who might survive the crossing. Your affinities make that possible. Not safe. Possible."

"You want me to go to Wyrmreach."

"I want you to consider it. There is something I need delivered."

"What?"

"An artifact."

"To whom?"

"Someone who can offer training I cannot."

Drusniel looked at the map. "I won't leave my family."

"Then don't." Zaelar's answer came without hesitation. "I'm asking you to consider a mission, not abandon them."

"Why now?"

"Because Umbra'kor is becoming less stable." He tapped one finger against the map. "Movement near contested territory. Houses shifting old alliances. Nothing decisive yet."

Drusniel thought of his father's tightened patrols. The dead informants. Shyntara watching every door.

"House Vrinn."

"Perhaps."

"If they move against my family, being stronger in Wyrmreach does nothing for them here."

"Strength gives you options. Knowledge gives you more. I suspect your family may need options soon."

Drusniel studied him. "And if I refuse?"

"Then you refuse."

"I need time," Drusniel said.

"Of course." Zaelar rolled the map closed. "A decision made under pressure is difficult to trust."

Drusniel looked at the warning marks across the sea. "You really think I could survive the crossing?"

"Could." Zaelar met his gaze. "Air and water give you a chance most drow do not have. If you decide to go, then we discuss how."
