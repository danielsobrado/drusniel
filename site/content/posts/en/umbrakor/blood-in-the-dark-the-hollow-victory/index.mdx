## Chapter 6 | Part 6
---

*Drusniel*

---

![Dusty storage chamber](./dusty-storage_sora.jpg)

Drusniel emerged into a disused storeroom. His shoulder brushed a stack of empty sacks, filling his mouth with dust. He spat and shut the tunnel door.

He sat against a wall until his breathing slowed.

Blood had dried on his hands. Some of it was his.

A spider crossed the floor and disappeared into a crack.

![Spider life](./spider-life_sora.jpg)

Drusniel stood.

---

The presence found him as he was climbing toward the surface passages.

*Drus.*

Familiar warmth brushed his awareness. He stopped climbing.

![Mental comfort](./mental-comfort_sora.jpg)

*Drus, are you alive? I felt something terrible. I've been trying to reach you for hours.*

He reached back. Pain tightened across his forehead, and he leaned against the wall until he could answer.

*I'm alive.*

*Thank the deep gods.* Relief came through the link, warm enough that Drusniel let himself believe it for a moment. *What happened? I felt pain, fear, then nothing. The connection just... stopped. I thought you were—*

*They're dead.*

Silence.

Then, gentler: *Your parents?*

*Everyone I found.* He could not make himself list them. *Vrinn weapons. Men who knew the house. They killed my father while I watched.*

*I'm sorry, Drus.* The warmth drew closer. *You shouldn't be alone now.*

Something in the timing was wrong.

*Meren was still alive when I found him.* Drusniel looked at his hand. *I couldn't stop the bleeding.*

*You need somewhere to rest. Somewhere they won't look first.*

Drusniel leaned into the comfort anyway.

*I have nowhere safe before daylight.*

*Go to Zaelar.*

*What?*

*Whatever else Zaelar is, he has food and somewhere to shelter you.*

Zaelar had offered Wyrmreach.

Power.

For the first time since the main hall, Drusniel knew what he wanted it for.

*What about you?* Drusniel asked. *If I go to Wyrmreach—*

*I'll be here.* The presence softened. *Get somewhere safe first.*

Drusniel touched the dagger at his hip.

*I'll go to Zaelar. Now.*

*Good.* The presence began to fade. *Be careful, Drus.*

The warmth withdrew.

The Vrinn blade was still clean. His hand had left blood on the hilt.

Drusniel started for Zaelar's tower.
