## Chapter 7 | Part 3
--- 

Drusniel woke to grey light behind closed shutters.

For one breath, he expected his mother's voice from downstairs.

Then he remembered.

He dressed in the surface clothes Zaelar had left for him and went down.

The metal plate waited at the center of the table.

![Opening the artifact case](./opening-box_sora.jpg)

It was smaller than his palm, geometric, covered in symbols that shifted when he tried to hold them in focus. Dark metal. Warm to the touch.

"What is it?"

"A tool," Zaelar said. "I call it the Null. It's old enough that I don't know who made the first one. It suppresses magical signature."

"Suppresses magic?"

"Signature. Not the same thing." Zaelar turned it over. "Wards detect the presence of power. This makes your presence difficult to distinguish from absence."

Drusniel stared at him.

"Show me."

Zaelar guided his thumb to one symbol.

"Press. Focus the way you do when you take hold of a current."

Drusniel pressed.

The world remained exactly where it was.

He did not.

![The object's surprising weight in hand](./weight-of-object_sora.jpg)

The absence hit his body before the memory did.

Trial chamber.

Magic reaching for something and finding nothing.

His thumb came off the symbol.

Air rushed back into his awareness—not physically. Magically. The constant pressure of currents against his senses returned.

Zaelar watched him.

"Again," Drusniel said.

Zaelar's brows lifted.

"Activate it again."

Drusniel pressed the symbol a second time.

The same absence.

He tried to catch the small draft coming beneath the door. He could still feel the air against his skin, but the magical sense behind it had gone distant, as if someone had moved the current to the other side of thick glass.

He released the plate.

"Where did you get this?"

"Wyrmreach."

"How long have you had it?"

"Longer than you've been alive."

Drusniel turned the plate over in his hands.

"Could something like this interfere with a trial chamber?"

Zaelar was silent for half a beat.

"Something that suppresses magical signature could interfere with many detection systems."

"Could this?"

"This plate was here."

"With you."

"Yes."

Drusniel tapped his thumb once against each fingertip.

A true answer could still conceal another one.

The voice that called itself Annariel had not appeared once during the test.

He stored that too.

"How long can I keep it active?"

"Briefly, if you want to remain functional. The cost rises with duration." Zaelar put the plate into a padded pouch. "During the sea crossing, keep it against your body. When it begins to pulse, the boundary wards are reacting. Follow the instructions I give you. Do not experiment then."

"Why not?"

"Because testing a mechanism while it is trying to kill you is poor methodology."

Drusniel almost smiled.

Almost.

Zaelar handed him the pouch.

"Szoravel will recognize its signal."

"You are certain?"

"Yes."

Another measurable claim.

Drusniel put the pouch into his pack beside the provisions. The Vrinn dagger remained at his belt.

"When do we leave?"

"At dusk. Until then, you stay behind the shutters."

Drusniel nodded.

The plate had reproduced the emptiness of his trial.

For the first time, that sensation had a mechanism attached to it.

He did not have an answer.

He had a better question.
